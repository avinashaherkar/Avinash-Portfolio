About

I cut for the way people actually watch — thumb on the screen, ready to keep scrolling.

I'm Avinash Aherkar, a short-form video editor working with brands and creators who need content that earns the first three seconds and holds attention through the last. Every cut, caption, and sound hit is placed to keep the thumb still.

My process blends fast-paced pacing with intentional pauses — pulling from music video editing, motion graphics, and platform-native trends to make content that feels native to the feed, not dropped into it.
